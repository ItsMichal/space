# Smash research: "Halmic" at SmashDEN

Researched 2026-10-11. Every fact has a source URL. Opponents are named by public tag only. Nothing here is shipped on the site (docs are not part of `public/`). The site's Smash block uses only what is listed below.

## What "SmashDEN" is

- A Super Smash Bros. Ultimate league/monthly tournament series in the Denver, Colorado area, 2018 to 2020 (at least). Free entry, monthly, streamed live on Twitch, brackets on start.gg (then smash.gg). Source: https://www.start.gg/tournament/smashden-february-2019/details and https://www.start.gg/league/smashden-2019
- Venue in 2019: The Tabletop Tap, Englewood, Colorado. Source: https://www.sponsormyevent.com/smashden-ultimate-league-englewood (the page names the organizer by full name; not copied here). start.gg lists every 2019 event as "Englewood, CO": https://www.start.gg/league/smashden-2019/schedule
- The 2019 league ended with the "SmashDEN - Invitational 2019", 7 Dec 2019, 32 players, Ultimate singles. Source: https://www.start.gg/league/smashden-2019/schedule (league text: points from monthly tournaments, season ends with an invitational: https://www.start.gg/league/smashden-2019)
- Monthly field sizes in 2019 were 43 to 64 players (same schedule page).
- The YouTube uploads of the sets come from the channel **YetiGamer** (https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCTT4_24mYMZJ3Sz_ni7RNYg), whose description links https://www.twitch.tv/yetigamerofficial. The league page says "We also have an online community at YetiGamer" (https://www.start.gg/league/smashden-2019), so YetiGamer is the organizer's brand and SmashDEN is the tournament series. SmashDEN's own Facebook page is https://www.facebook.com/smashden.yetigamer/ and its Twitter is linked as https://twitter.com/_yetigamerofficial (from the sponsormyevent page above).
- Not to be confused with: the YouTube handle `@smashden`, which is an unrelated channel titled "Den Den" (https://www.youtube.com/@smashden). A later start.gg series "SmashDen #2", 17 Jun 2023, Highlands Ranch, CO ("monthly Arcadian Ultimate Singles tournament series") also exists: https://www.start.gg/tournament/smashden-2-1/details . It is not clear it is the same organizer; none of the VODs below are from it.
- Region/scene: Colorado (Denver metro). The same Colorado scene appears in the 2019 YouTube search results as "Colorado Smash Ultimate PR 2019 Q1" (https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=SmashDEN+Denver+Colorado+smash+ultimate), listed only as scene context.

## Identity: is this his Halmic?

Evidence, all public:
1. The owner's own YouTube channel hosts "CyberSmash #0" sets (a Colorado school club tournament, 2019 era; the video description says it took place at a school in Highlands Ranch) where he is tagged "Halmic [Snake]" and "Halmic [Ike]": https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUtLZU4pik-NHfJJ3InmHxeKGH9nTqyZ3 (now removed from halmic.com by the owner's decision). Same tag, same state, same game, same period.
2. In every SmashDEN set overlay/thumbnail checked (six of the eight VODs), the "Halmic" side plays Snake: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3-fIoRP40_A , https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GdsFqsdzX9M , https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uiDxlLdA4MQ , https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hMO-ZjR9GkE , https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dcOZ7mDQ5d0 , https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwW1XOMIb-k . This matches "Halmic [Snake]" in CyberSmash.
3. Time period 2019 to early 2020 matches the owner's school and Smash years (CyberSmash was 2019 era).

Confidence the SmashDEN "Halmic" is the owner: high on tag + state + character + period, but the owner must confirm (nothing publishes a real-name link, and none was copied). I found no start.gg profile page for the tag through search: the player profile is not indexed (start.gg is a JavaScript app; its player search was not usable without logging in).
