About the game

Phoenix Rising is a free, fan-made Pokémon RPG, built with RPG Maker XP and Pokémon Essentials and released in episodes. Episode 1 came out in 2018. The team shares trailers on the game’s YouTube channel and talks with players on its subreddit. It is a fan project, not made by or affiliated with Nintendo, Game Freak or The Pokémon Company.
